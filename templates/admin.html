Photo storage

Phone photos are often 3 to 15 MB. By default, large photos are turned the right way up and saved as high-quality JPEGs, at most 4096 pixels on the long side, with full colour detail. A 12 megapixel photo takes about 40% of the space and a 48 megapixel one about a tenth, with no visible difference; in tests the plate analysis read the same colony coverage. The phone's location data is removed. Photos already stored are not changed (the server command manage.py compact-photos does that).
